BMR is what you'd burn lying in bed all day — about 1,690 kcal at 73 kg. The multiplier is everything on top of that: the cycle commute, the gym, football, walking around. Multiply the two and you get what you actually burn in a day.

1.66 is the current figure. For scale: 1.375 is a desk job with no training, 1.55 is three or four sessions a week, 1.9 is an athlete training twice a day.

⚠ It is the least trustworthy number in the app. The old record used 1.7, which assumes hard exercise six or seven days a week, against a logged reality of one gym session a week — that single wrong figure overstated the target by 250–400 kcal, the entire size of the intended deficit. The honest error band is still ±250–400 kcal.

So don't tune it by feel — settle it with the scale. Weigh in fasted, and after four weeks: down more than 0.5 kg, add 150 kcal. Up more than 1.0 kg, take 150 off. Anywhere between, it's right. That rule beats any adjustment made from a formula.

Where the log lives

This app commits every closed day to data/health-daily-log.csv in a GitHub repo. The repo is the database — each logged day is a commit, so the history is the audit trail and nothing can be silently overwritten.
⚠ Use a fine-grained personal access token scoped to this one repository, with Contents: read and write and nothing else. It is stored in this browser only, on this device. It is a real secret sitting in browser storage — that is the weak point of this design, and it is not pretended otherwise.
